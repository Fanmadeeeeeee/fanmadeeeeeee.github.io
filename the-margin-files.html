About the channel

The Margin Files is an English-language documentary channel on YouTube about overlooked people, forgotten inventions, strange true events, and the hidden mechanisms behind famous moments and everyday things.

TuesdayUntold Story — a real person or event that history skipped.
ThursdayHidden Mechanism — how something famous or everyday really works behind the scenes.
SaturdayCase File — a strange true mystery, and what is actually known.

Every fact is checked against reputable sources (archives, museums, journals, major newspapers), and the sources are listed under each video. The channel does not publish conspiracy theories. Narration uses an AI voice; the animation and music are original. The channel is not affiliated with the companies, institutions or people its videos describe.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

This policy describes how Margin Files Uploader ("the app") handles data. The app is a private publishing tool operated by the owner of The Margin Files YouTube channel. It is used only by the channel owner to manage that one channel. It has no other users and no public sign-in.

YouTube API Services

The app uses the YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What the app accesses

With the channel owner's authorization (Google OAuth), the app:

  • uploads the channel's own videos and sets their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and publishing schedule;
  • creates and updates the channel's own playlists;
  • reads the channel's own basic information (name and ID) to confirm the connection;
  • may read the channel's own aggregated performance statistics (YouTube Analytics) to improve future videos;
  • reads public information about public YouTube videos (titles, view counts) to research topics.

The app does not access any other user's private data, subscribers' personal information, or any private data outside this one channel.

What the app stores

  • The IDs of videos and playlists it created, kept in the channel owner's private storage so the same video is not uploaded twice.
  • The OAuth credentials, stored only in the channel owner's private, access-restricted environment and never shared or published.

Aggregated statistics, if read, are used only to plan the channel's next videos and are not shared.

Sharing

The app does not sell, rent or share any data with third parties, and does not use data for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Stored video and playlist IDs are kept while the channel operates and can be deleted at any time by the owner. Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account → Security → Third-party connections; revoking access stops the app immediately.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from viewers.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
